Ghanim Yousef Al-Shaheen

born 1923

Life facts

Born
1923, Kuwait
Education
Studied at Al-Mubarakiya School through all its stages, 1928 to 1939
Sea
Sailed on Shaheen Al-Ghanim's ship from 1940 until the end of the Second World War in 1945
Sea
Active member of the Al-Irshad Al-Islami society, where he also taught literacy classes. Deputy editor and then editor in chief of the Daily News, then gave his time to writing books on Kuwait's heritage, among them «Al-Kuwait wal Madhi Al-Areeq», «Al-Kuwaitiyoun wa Lahjatuhum Al-Jameela», «Kuwaitna Jawharatuna», «Seerat Tifl Al-Ams», «Al-Kuwait Barruha wa Bahruha» and «Watan wa Wataniya»
Source
Al Zayed book, Yousef Mohammed Al-Jasser Al-Ghanim, 2006
