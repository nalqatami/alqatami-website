Khalifa Shaheen Al-Ghanim

1875 to 1953

Life in brief

Khalifa bin Shaheen Al-Ghanim was born in Sharq in 1875 and learned the sea from his father Shaheen, one of the family's leading nakhudas, before turning to trade. Known for his sound judgement, he sat on Kuwait's first Shura Council in 1921, the first Municipal Council and the first Education Council, and settled disputes between nakhudas. Alan Villiers praised his knowledge, and his sons carried on commanding his ships. He died in 1953.

Life facts

Born
1875, Sharq, Kuwait
Died
1953
Occupation
A skilled nakhuda and a successful merchant, and a reference on Kuwait's history in his time. Captain Alan Villiers praised what he knew in his 1938 book on the Sinbad voyage
Sons
The book names his sons Abdulwahab, Ali and Suleiman as nakhudas who carried on commanding his ships, and its chart also lists Eissa and Fahad
Sea
Learned seamanship from his father, Shaheen Al-Ghanim, one of the family's leading nakhudas, alongside his brothers Mohammed, Jassim and Abdulrahman. He did not command for long, though he did take over his father's ship «Anbar Taweel», and soon turned to trade
Public life
Known for sound judgement and fairness. Member of the first Shura Council in 1921, the first Municipal Council and the first Education Council. With his brother Mohammed and their friend Barrak bin Khamis he settled disputes between nakhudas
Source
Al Zayed book, Yousef Mohammed Al-Jasser Al-Ghanim, 2006, pages 19 to 21 and 55
