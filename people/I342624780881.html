Yacoub Yousef Al-Qatami

1927 to 2014

Life in brief

Yacoub bin Yousef bin Eissa Al-Qatami was born in Sharq in 1927 and studied political science and economics at Cairo University in the 1950s. He was offered the post of Kuwait's first delegate to the United Nations but declined, because his father needed him to run the family business, which he led from the early 1960s until the mid 1990s, building schools, hospitals and fuel stations. He was a founding member of the Kuwait Investment Company, sat on the board of the Central Bank of Kuwait for two terms, and died in 2014.

Life facts

Born
1927, Sharq, Kuwait
Died
2014. He left two sons, two daughters and thirteen grandchildren
Education
Studied political science and economics at Cairo University in the 1950s. He was offered the post of Kuwait's first delegate to the United Nations, as one of the first qualified for it, but declined because his father needed him to run his business
Education
Studied in Cairo; photographed with the Kuwaiti students there in 1952 (Rijal fi Tarikh Al-Kuwait, part 1), Cairo, Egypt
Occupation
Ran the business of his father, Nakhuda Yousef bin Eissa Al-Qatami, in transport, contracting and industry under the Al-Ashghal wal Binaa establishment, from the early 1960s until his father's death in 1981, and then until he retired in the mid 1990s. He began with transport in Ahmadi for the oil companies, then built schools, hospitals and fuel stations. He persuaded his father to set up one of the first factories for insulation materials, still working today, and invested in a large prefabricated housing factory in the early 1970s whose output reached as far as Iran, Iraq and Saudi Arabia
Public roles
Founding member representing the government in setting up the Kuwait Investment Company; member of the board of the Central Bank of Kuwait for two terms; chairman of the committee to found the iron and steel factory
Source
Biography written by his son Wael Yacoub Al-Qatami in 2020, posted by alghanim_history, 7 July 2025
