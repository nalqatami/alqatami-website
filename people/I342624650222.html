Abdulaziz Abdulwahab Abdulaziz Al-Qatami

died 1941

Life in brief

Nakhuda Abdulaziz bin Abdulwahab Al-Qatami commanded the baghla Al-Badri, and Sheikh Mubarak chose him to bring the yacht Mushrif, Kuwait's first steamship, home from Bombay. He later traded with India by steamer, was so devout that he was called «the dove of the mosque», gave to build the Al-Qarawiya mosque in 1915, and died in 1360 AH (1941). His house stood beside the mosque that came to carry the family's name.

Life facts

Died
1360 AH (1941 or early 1942), court heirs record, Kuwait
Sea
He was among the great nakhudas of Kuwait gathered around Sheikh Mubarak Al-Sabah at the launch of Abdulaziz Al-Othman's boom Tayseer (Dr Abdulmohsen Abdullah Al-Kharafi, Al-Othman: Madrasat Al-Safar Al-Shira'i fil-Kuwait, page 209)
Charity
On 20 Jumada I 1334 AH (1915) «Abdulaziz bin Qatami» is listed among the Kuwaiti merchants who gave towards building the Al-Qarawiya Mosque, with 20 rupees (notebook published by Torathona)
Trade
Listed as «’Abdul ’Aziz Bin Qatami» among the dealers in grains and pulses in Kuwait in The Indian Trade Journal, volume 71, Kuwait
