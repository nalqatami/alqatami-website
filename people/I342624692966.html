Ahmed Abdulaziz Al-Qatami

born 1922

Life in brief

Ahmed bin Abdulaziz bin Abdulwahab Al-Qatami was born in Sharq in 1922. He married Monira bint Hamad bin Abdullah Al-Saqr, and their daughters include Khalda and Wafa. In 1957 he was a witness to the heirs declaration of his wife's half sister, Fadda bint Hamad Al-Saqr.

Life facts

Born
1922, Sharq (his daughter Khalda, Al-Anba, 2008), Kuwait
Witness
In 1957 he was a witness to the heirs declaration of Fadda bint Hamad bin Abdullah Al-Saqr: «Ahmed bin Abdulaziz Al-Qatami and Abdullah bin Ahmed Al-Hasawi testified that Fadda bint Hamad bin Abdullah Al-Saqr died on 12 Rabi Al-Akhir 1352 (4 August 1933), survived by her mother Fatima bint Suleiman Al-Bassam and her half brothers and sisters Abdullah, Abdulaziz, Mohammed, Jassim, Abdulwahab, Shaha, Fatima, Tayba, Noura and Monira» (heirs declaration no. 163, 11 May 1957, quoted in Ma'alim Madinat Al-Kuwait Al-Qadima, part 8, page 181)
Married
On the evening of Thursday 19 April 1945 (Ahmed Al-Bishr Al-Roumi’s diary, p. 107), Kuwait
