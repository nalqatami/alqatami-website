Abdulrahman Abdulaziz Al-Qatami

died 1995

Life in brief

Nakhuda Abdulrahman bin Abdulaziz Al-Qatami went to sea because his father wished it. On his first command he ran aground near Ras Kuh, and he gave up sailing around 1937. His nephew Sager remembered him as «Abdulrahman Al-Qatami, who baffled the pirates». He died in 1995.

Life facts

Died
1995 (Al-Hajji, Nawakhdhat Al-Safar Al-Shirai, p. 59), Kuwait
Sea
His nephew Sager Abdulwahab Al-Qatami named him among the family's nakhudas as «Abdulrahman Al-Qatami, who baffled the pirates» (Ma'alim Madinat Al-Kuwait Al-Qadima, part 7, page 43, quoting Al-Qabas, 25 January 2012)
Sea
June 1943: sailed from Karachi and reached Kuwait on 15 June. August 1945: travelled from Basra to Bombay by steamship (Al-Roumi diary, pp. 97, 98, 111 and 112)
Married
Thajba Sanad Al-Fadala. Named in Ma’alim Madinat Al-Kuwait Al-Qadima, part 8, and confirmed by the family in November 2026
Friends
A close friend of the writer Ahmed Al-Bishr Al-Roumi, whose diaries name him often between 1943 and 1978 (Ahmed Al-Bishr Al-Roumi: Qira’a fi Awraqihi Al-Khassa, ed. Dr Yacoub Yousef Al-Ghunaim, 1997)
Children
Al-Roumi wrote that a daughter, Bassiqa (written Bazgha in the diary), was born to him on 16 February 1945, about half an hour before sunset (diary, p. 106)
Lived
In March and April 1978 he visited his friend Al-Roumi again and again in hospital in London (diary, pp. 420 to 431), London, England, United Kingdom
