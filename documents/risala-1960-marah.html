«The freedom of the Kuwaiti woman»: Jassim Al-Qatami in Al-Risala, 1960

Jassim Abdulaziz Al-Qatami

What it is

The weekly investigation «The freedom of the Kuwaiti woman» asked ministers and public figures for their views on women’s freedom, education, work and women’s societies. Page 11 carries a photograph captioned «Mr Jassim Al-Qatami» among those whose views were published.

Why it matters

Jassim Abdulaziz Al-Qatami taking part, in 1960, in the public debate on the place of women in Kuwait.

Who is meant

The paper names him only as «Mr Jassim Al-Qatami». He is identified as Jassim Abdulaziz Al-Qatami, who was active in public life in Kuwait at that time.
