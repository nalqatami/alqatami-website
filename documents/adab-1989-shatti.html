Jassim Al-Qatami among the first writers of the Kuwaiti short story

Jassim Abdulaziz Al-Qatami

What it is

Dr Suleiman Al-Shatti names Jassim Al-Qatami as one of the two writers who set the course of the Kuwaiti short story. Al-Qatami focused on the sea: his three attempts were «A Sailor’s End», «A Sailor’s Marriage» and «A Sailor’s Diary». Al-Shatti calls him «a man of political leaning» whose main concern was social criticism and reform, siding with the toiling sailors against exploitation. His biography is cited from Khalid Saud Al-Zaid, Udaba’ Al-Kuwait fi Qarnayn, volume 3, pages 303 to 354.

Why it matters

A literary historian places Jassim’s 1948 sea stories at the start of the Kuwaiti short story.
