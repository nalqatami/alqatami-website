1975 · British Embassy reports on the National Assembly session

Jassim Al-Qatami raises Article 98 of the Constitution

Jassim Abdulaziz Al-Qatami

What it is

Jassem Qatami rises on a point of order and accuses the Cabinet of breaking Article 98 of the Constitution by not presenting a programme for its term. Several deputies back him, and he is among the sponsors of a motion to send the matter to the Legal and Legislative Affairs Committee.

Why it matters

A record of Jassim's work inside the Assembly, defending the Constitution.

Who is meant

The same Jassim Al-Qatami as the records that give his full name: Director of Police, resigned at Suez, later a nationalist member of the National Assembly.

Page copies

Original text

Page 51: … when Jassem Qatami rose to speak on a point of order. He accused the Cabinet of having committed a constitutional irregularity by failing to apply Article 98 of the Constitution and said that the Article had been inoperative for the last 12 years. He added that the article provided for any new Cabinet to present to the Assembly a policy statement setting out its programme for its four-year tenure of office. He said that the Amiri Speech could not be regarded as a programme because it was merely a review of the Government's activities and achievements and that it was covered by another article of the Constitution: Article 104. 3. A number of deputies spoke, taking turns at supporting Qatami's argument. Page 52: The sponsors of the motion were Jassem Qatami, Rashid Farhan, Habib Hayat, Jan'an Huraithi, Jassem Sattan, Khaled Khalaf and Jassem Kharafi.

English translation

الصفحة ٥١: … حين وقف جاسم القطامي على نقطة نظام، واتهم مجلس الوزراء بارتكاب مخالفة دستورية بعدم تطبيق المادة ٩٨ من الدستور، وقال إن المادة معطلة منذ اثني عشر عاما. وأضاف أن المادة توجب على كل وزارة جديدة أن تقدم إلى المجلس بيانا ببرنامجها لمدة ولايتها البالغة أربع سنوات. وقال إن الخطاب الأميري لا يمكن اعتباره برنامجا لأنه مجرد استعراض لنشاطات الحكومة وإنجازاتها، وتغطيه مادة أخرى من الدستور هي المادة ١٠٤. ٣. وتحدث عدد من النواب، يؤيدون حجة القطامي واحدا بعد آخر. الصفحة ٥٢: ومقدمو الاقتراح هم جاسم القطامي وراشد الفرحان وحبيب حياة وجنعان الهريطي وجاسم الصطام وخالد خلف وجاسم الخرافي.

Notes

Page 29 of the same file also names Qatami among the left wing who kept up pressure on the Government. Names of other deputies are as the British spelled them.
