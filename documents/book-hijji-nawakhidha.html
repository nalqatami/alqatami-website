Pages from Dr Yacoub Al-Hijji's The Captains of Kuwait's Sailing Voyages

Abdulwahab Abdulaziz Al-Qatami

What it is

Page 403: in 1936 Nakhuda Jassim Al-Mubarak left the command of Al-Daw and Nakhuda Abdulwahab bin Abdulaziz Al-Qatami took it. Page 401: in 1936, when Ali Al-Mubarak's ship ran aground at Ras Laffan, Kuwait sent a party led by Abdulwahab Al-Qatami that repaired it.

Why it matters

The standard reference on Kuwait's sailing captains.

Page copies

Notes

Page 401 does not say which Abdulwahab Al-Qatami led the rescue, so it is not linked to a profile. Only the first pages are shown here. Family members who sign in can see all 3 pages.
