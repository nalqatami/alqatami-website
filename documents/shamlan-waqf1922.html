28 Shawwal 1340 (24 June 1922) · Waqf deed after a land dispute, under Sheikh Ahmad Al-Jaber

Shamlan bin Ali bin Saif's waqf deed: Masjid Ibn Rumi «in the Ibn Qatami quarter»

Abdulaziz Al-Qatami

What it is

Shamlan bin Ali bin Saif and Mufarrej walad Mashouh disputed a walled plot in Barahat Mujbil. The case went to Sheikh Ahmad Al-Jaber, who sent them to the judge. The plot was proven to be Shamlan's, inherited from his grandfather, and once it was settled in his hands he endowed it to the mosque now called Masjid Ibn Rumi, «in the Ibn Qatami quarter».

Why it matters

It shows that in 1922 the whole quarter around Masjid Ibn Rumi was officially called after our family, «محلة ابن قطامي». Ma'alim part 7 uses the same name for Fereej Ibn Rumi, where the Al-Qatami houses and Masjid Al-Qatami stood.

Who is meant

No family member is named in this deed. It is here because it names the quarter after the family: محلة ابن قطامي.

Original text

السبب الداعي إلى تحرير هذه الأحرف الشرعية هو أنه لما تداعا شملان ابن علي ابن سيف مع مفرح ولد مشوح على الحوطة الواقعة في براحة مجبل المحدودة قبلتا بيت عبدالرحمن ابن تويتان وشمالا الحفرة وشرقا الطريق العام وجنوبا بيت مفرح ولد مشوح المذكور، وانتهت دعواهما إلى حضرة المكرم الشيخ أحمد ابن المرحوم الشيخ جابر المبارك، وأحضرهما لدى … الشرع الشريف الموقع اسمه وختمه على الكتاب، فلما حضرا تداعيا عليها، ثبت بعد الدعوى أنها لشملان ملكا لجده حسين … واستخلصها من يد مفرح بعد الدعوى ورفع مفرح يده عنها، فلما استقرت في يد شملان وفي ملكه أوقفها على مؤذن … المسمى الآن مسجد ابن رومي الواقع في محلة ابن قطامي وقفا صحيحا شرعيا منجزا لا تباع ولا تورث ولا توهب ولا ترهن، فمن بدله بعد ما سمعه فإنما إثمه على الذين يبدلونه. جرا وحرر في ٢٨ شوال سنة ١٣٤٠

English translation

The reason for writing these lawful lines is that Shamlan ibn Ali ibn Saif and Mufarrej walad Mashouh disputed the walled plot in Barahat Mujbil, bounded on the west by the house of Abdulrahman ibn Tuwaitan, on the north by the pit, on the east by the main road, and on the south by the house of the said Mufarrej walad Mashouh. Their dispute went to His Honour Sheikh Ahmad, son of the late Sheikh Jaber Al-Mubarak, who brought them before the judge whose name and seal are on this document. When they appeared and argued over it, it was proven that it belonged to Shamlan, having been owned by his grandfather Hussain … He recovered it from Mufarrej after the case, and Mufarrej gave up his claim. Once it was settled in Shamlan's hands and ownership, he endowed it for the muezzin of … now called Masjid Ibn Rumi, located in the Ibn Qatami quarter, as a valid, lawful and binding waqf, not to be sold, inherited, given away or mortgaged. Whoever changes it after hearing it, the sin is on those who change it. Written on 28 Shawwal 1340.
