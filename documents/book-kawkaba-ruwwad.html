Kawkaba min Al-Ruwwad: Masjid Al-Qatami, founded by Malka Al-Ghanim and rebuilt in 1953

Malka Mohammad Al-Ghanim

What it is

1. Page 51: «Masjid Al-Qatami. Founded by Malka bint Mohammed Ghanim Al-Jabr Al-Ghanim in 1250 AH (1834) for her husband to pray in, and it was called Masjid Al-Qatami because it is near the house of Abdulaziz Al-Qatami.» It stands in Sharq, in Fereej Al-Shamlan or Al-Roumi, between Fereej Buqmaz and Fereej Al-Asousi, behind the Bishr Al-Roumi building, near the house of Abdulaziz Al-Qatami. It was first enlarged by the people of the quarter on its west side, with a larger mihrab; the Awqaf Department rebuilt it on 10 Dhul Hijja 1372 AH (20 August 1953); and the General Secretariat of Awqaf was restoring it under its heritage mosques project. With a photograph of the mosque. 2. Page 41, the mosques built by the Awqaf Department in Kuwait Town: Masjid Ibn Qatami, 10/12/1372 AH, at a cost of 66,924 rupees. 3. Page 45, the mosques and their locations: Masjid Ibn Qatami, in Al-Roumi.

Why it matters

The Awqaf Secretariat's own account of Masjid Al-Qatami: its founding by Malka Al-Ghanim in 1834, the origin of its name in the house of Abdulaziz Al-Qatami, and its rebuilding by the state in 1953.

Who is meant

The book does not say which Abdulaziz Al-Qatami owned the house, so he is not linked to a person in the tree.

How this text was made: Page 41 gives the rebuilding date as 10/12/1372 AH with the Gregorian date 31/8/1953; page 51 gives 20 August 1953. Page 44 does not name the family, so it is not shown.
