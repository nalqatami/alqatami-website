November 1956 · British report on the internal political situation in the Gulf

Jassim Al-Qatami joins the Committee of the Clubs

Jassim Abdulaziz Al-Qatami

What it is

The report says the Committee of the Clubs, helped by people in the Education Department, was strengthened when Colonel Jasim Qatami, formerly director of the police, came over to their cause at the start of the troubles.

Why it matters

It shows Jassim moving straight from the police into the reform movement in 1956.

Who is meant

The same Jassim Al-Qatami as the records that give his full name: Director of Police, resigned at Suez, later a nationalist member of the National Assembly.

Page copies

Original text

Meanwhile, the Committee of the Clubs, assisted by many elements in the Department of Education including the Director, Abdul Aziz Husain, and strengthened by the defection to their cause at the beginning of the troubles of the prominent young Kuwaiti, Colonel Jasim Qatami, formerly director of the police, have continued their efforts in support of the demands mentioned above.

English translation

وفي الأثناء، واصلت لجنة الأندية جهودها دعما للمطالب المذكورة أعلاه، بمساعدة كثير من العناصر في دائرة المعارف ومنهم مديرها عبدالعزيز حسين، وقد تعززت بانضمام الشاب الكويتي البارز العقيد جاسم القطامي، مدير الشرطة السابق، إلى قضيتها في بداية الأحداث.
