1348 to 1350 AH (1929 to 1931) · Typed list of boats, owners and the dates of their papers

A list of Kuwaiti boats and their owners: Fath Al-Khair and Al-Badri

Abdulaziz Abdulwahab Abdulaziz Al-QatamiAbdulwahab Abdulaziz Al-Qatami

What it is

Row 62: the boom Fath Al-Khair, owner Abdulaziz bin Abdulwahab bin Qatami, 2 Jumada I 1348 AH (1929). Row 63: the baghla Al-Badri, owner Abdulwahab bin Abdulaziz bin Qatami, 28 Rabi I 1350 AH (1931).

Why it matters

Father and son appear one after the other, each with his own ship. The baghla Al-Badri is the same ship named in the Al-Badri letters on this page.

Who is meant

Both names carry the full chain: Abdulaziz bin Abdulwahab, and his son Abdulwahab bin Abdulaziz.
