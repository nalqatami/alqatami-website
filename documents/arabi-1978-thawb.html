«The Kuwaiti Thawb Is Half a Century Old», by Lolwa Al-Qatami

Lolwa Abdulwahab Al-Qatami

What it is

Lolwa Al-Qatami describes the old Kuwaiti woman’s thawb: its cut, fabrics and gold embroidery, its names (thawb tel, thawb mtel, thawb jaz), how women wore it until the 1950s, and the women’s societies’ campaign to keep it alive. She writes that when the society took the Kuwaiti thawb to a women’s conference in Moscow in 1967, it drew everyone’s attention.

Why it matters

An article by Lolwa herself in the leading Arab magazine of the time.
