What it is

Dr Al-Hijji's book on Kuwait's sea customs and stories, translated by Yousuf Al-Bader and Sayed Issawi Ayoub. It tells stories he heard from Bader Abdulwahab Al-Qatami himself in his office near Seef Palace, stories of Abdulwahab bin Abdulaziz, Abdulwahab Eissa's 1938 British certificate, Naser's words about the sea, and the booms Jerman and Jaban owned by Al-Qatami.

Why it matters

First hand stories of the family's nakhudas, some told to the author by Bader himself.
