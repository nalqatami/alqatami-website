6 Shawwal, in the ledger begun Sha'ban 1912 · Handwritten ledger of Sheikh Mubarak Al-Sabah for the people of the pearl dive (cover and one page)

Pearl diving dues paid through Abdulwahab Al-Qatami, in Sheikh Mubarak Al-Sabah’s diving ledger

What it is

The cover reads: «This is a ledger set aside for the people of the dive, belonging to His Highness our master Sheikh Mubarak Al-Sabah», beginning in the month of Sha'ban, with 1912 written beneath, and signed by its writer, Jassim bin Mohammed. The page dated 6 Shawwal, headed «belonging to my master Sheikh Mubarak Al-Sabah», lists the sums received from each crew, carried over from page 69. Its first line records 126 rupees received from Abdulaziz bin Fahad «by the hand of Abdulwahab Al-Qatami».

Why it matters

An Abdulwahab Al-Qatami handling diving season dues paid to Sheikh Mubarak, in a ledger written in the Sheikh’s own office.
