1970 · Secret letter from the British Ambassador in Kuwait, copied to the Gulf embassies

Jassim Al-Qatami as a "moderate evolutionary"

Jassim Abdulaziz Al-Qatami

What it is

The Ambassador gives Abdulaziz Hussain and Jassim Qatami as Kuwait's examples of moderate evolutionaries, people who wanted steady reform, and wishes the Sabah would work with them.

Why it matters

A changed British view: by 1970 Jassim is described as a moderate.

Who is meant

The same Jassim Al-Qatami as the records that give his full name: Director of Police, resigned at Suez, later a nationalist member of the National Assembly.

Page copies

Original text

5. You may ask me to give examples of moderate evolutionaries. The outstanding one, of course, is Abdul Rahman al Bazzaz and those who supported him in Iraq. We have them in Kuwait in the shape of Abdul Aziz Hussain (Leading Personalities in Kuwait 1970, No. 33) and Jassim Qatami (No. 61), and some of the bright educated youth. … I wish the Sabah would do this in Kuwait, and the Crown Prince's telecast does seem to offer a tiny ray of hope.

English translation

٥. قد تطلب مني أمثلة على التطوريين المعتدلين. أبرزهم بالطبع عبدالرحمن البزاز ومن أيّدوه في العراق. ولدينا منهم في الكويت عبدالعزيز حسين (الشخصيات البارزة في الكويت ١٩٧٠، رقم ٣٣) وجاسم القطامي (رقم ٦١)، وبعض الشباب المتعلم اللامع. … وأتمنى لو يفعل آل الصباح ذلك في الكويت، ويبدو أن كلمة ولي العهد المتلفزة تقدم بصيص أمل صغيرا.
