Eissa Al-Qatami quoted on the dangers of anchoring in the Gulf of Khambhat, in «Turuq Al-Tijara Al-Bahriya»

Eissa Abdulwahab Al-Qatami

What it is

An article on the old sea trade routes of the Arabian Gulf and the Indian Ocean. On page 114, after Al-Mas'udi's account of the tides in the Gulf of Khambhat (Cambay), it says: «Eissa Al-Qatami, one of the later sailors of the Arabian Gulf, describes the dangers of anchoring in the ports of the Gulf of Khambhat», and quotes him: if you anchor a ship close to the shore, the currents there are very strong and pour like a flood in a wadi, and a single anchor will not hold the ship unless it is a large one; and that at the head of the creek at low tide hardly any water remains, so a ship should keep clear of it. The notes on pages 117 and 118 cite Eissa Al-Qatami, Dalil Al-Muhtar fi Ilm Al-Bihar, fourth edition, pages 80 (note 18) and 118 (note 46).

Why it matters

A study of Arab navigation quotes Eissa’s Dalil Al-Muhtar as a working seaman’s authority beside Al-Mas'udi and Ibn Majid.
