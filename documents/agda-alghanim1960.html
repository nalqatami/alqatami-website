13 January 1960 · Secret letter from the British Political Agency in Kuwait to the Foreign Office

Jassim Al-Qatami's new business partnership with the Alghanims

Jassim Abdulaziz Al-Qatami

What it is

The Agency comments on a British diplomat's conversation with Jasim Qatami. It says he was putting much of his money into a new business partnership with one of the Alghanims, and gives a British view of his standing among the reformists.

Why it matters

It records a business tie between Jassim and the Al-Ghanim family, who are closely joined to the Al-Qatami tree.

Who is meant

The same Jassim Al-Qatami as the records that give his full name: Director of Police, resigned at Suez, later a nationalist member of the National Assembly.

Page copies

Original text

Political Agency, Kuwait. January 13, 1960. Secret. Thank you for sending a copy of Alan Rothnie's Minute of his conversation with Jasim Qatami. 2. Qatami was expressing his standard line. As usual, he exaggerates. For one thing, he personally is not sending all his money to the "Nationalist forces" in Iraq, but putting much of it into a new business partnership he has established with one of the Alghanims. 3. All our indications for the last few months have been that Qatami is less and less representative of reformist opinion, which itself is divided and disorganised at present …

English translation

الوكالة السياسية، الكويت. ١٣ يناير ١٩٦٠. سري. شكرا لإرسالك نسخة من محضر آلن روثني عن محادثته مع جاسم القطامي. ٢. كان القطامي يعبّر عن موقفه المعتاد، ويبالغ كعادته. فهو مثلا لا يرسل كل أمواله إلى «القوى القومية» في العراق، بل يضع كثيرا منها في شراكة تجارية جديدة أسسها مع أحد آل الغانم. ٣. كل مؤشراتنا في الأشهر الأخيرة تدل على أن القطامي يمثل الرأي الإصلاحي بدرجة أقل فأقل، وهذا الرأي نفسه منقسم وغير منظم حاليا …
