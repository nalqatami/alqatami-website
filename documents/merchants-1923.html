1923 · A printed British list, «Dealers in Grains and Pulses»

Abdulaziz bin Qatami among the grain merchants of Kuwait, 1923

Abdulaziz Abdulwahab Abdulaziz Al-Qatami

What it is

Under «5. Kuwait», the list of dealers in grains and pulses names «'Abdul 'Aziz Bin Qatami» among the town's merchants.

Why it matters

It shows Abdulaziz bin Abdulwahab trading in grain as well as sailing.

Who is meant

Abdulaziz bin Abdulwahab Al-Qatami, the only Abdulaziz bin Qatami trading in Kuwait at that time.
