22 December 1953 · Merchant's letter, as transcribed and published in Al-Jarida

Letter from Ahmad Al-Yousef Al-Nisf and Brothers: a kotiya cargo sold to Naser bin Qatami

Naser Abdulwahab Al-Qatami

What it is

The Nisf brothers in Kuwait write to the merchant Hamad Al-Abdulmohsen Al-Khamis, who was supplying goods from India. They confirm a telegraph transfer of 10,000 to Yousef Al-Saqer, and report that they sold the cargo of a kotiya ship to Naser bin Qatami at twelve and a half per ten, part payable when the goods arrive and the rest within three months. The ship had not yet reached Kuwait; they were waiting for it with the north wind blowing in the Arabian Gulf.

Why it matters

It shows Naser Abdulwahab Al-Qatami as a merchant in Kuwait in 1953, buying a whole ship's cargo from India on credit.

Who is meant

The letter names only Naser bin Qatami. The family identifies him as Naser Abdulwahab Al-Qatami.

Original text

حضرة الاجل المكرم الحاج حمد العبدالمحسن الخميس المحترم بعد التحية، نرجو أن تكون بخير... أخذنا جميع مكاتيبكم، وآخرهم برقيتكم التي تطلبون فيها تحويل عشرة آلاف إلى يوسف الصقر. بوقته حوّلنا للمذكور بالتلغراف 10000 (عشرة آلاف)، نأمل استلامها بوقته، وأشعركم بذلك. الكوتية صرفناها العشر اثنتعشر ونصف ودفع الدراهم عند استلام المال يدفع لتاريخ المبلغ والباقي مدة ثلاثة أشهر، وهذا أحسن سعر حصلناه، والحقيقة توفقنا في بيعها، وقد صرفناها على ناصر بن قطامي، منتظرين وصولها إن شاء الله قريب، حيث الهوى شمال في الخليج، هذا ما لزم منا الإخوان يسلمون سلامنا العائلة عموم ودمتم.

English translation

To the honourable Haj Hamad Al-Abdulmohsen Al-Khamis, respected, After greetings, we hope you are well. We have received all your letters, the last of them your telegram asking us to transfer ten thousand to Yousef Al-Saqer. We transferred it to him at once by telegraph, 10,000 (ten thousand); we hope he received it in time, and we let you know. We sold the kotiya cargo at twelve and a half per ten, the money to be paid on receipt of the goods, part on the date of the sum and the rest over three months. This is the best price we got, and truly we did well in selling it. We sold it to Naser bin Qatami, and we are waiting for it to arrive soon, God willing, as the wind is blowing from the north in the Gulf. That is all from us. The brothers send their greetings to the whole family. Stay well.

Notes

Al-Jarida published this letter on 4 November 2016 from the papers of Hamad Al-Abdulmohsen Al-Khamis, kept by his son Abdulmohsen. The paper says Abdullatif Al-Nisf appears to have signed it. The original letter itself is not shown here, only the published text.
