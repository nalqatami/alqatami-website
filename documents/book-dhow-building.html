What it is

Dr Al-Hijji's chapter on Kuwait's renowned dhows gives the histories of three ships of the family. Al-Badri, built about 1880 by master builder Muhammad Grouf, was the school ship of Abdulwahab Al-Qatami and his sons and was sold at Aden on 14 December 1941. Al-Dhow, built in 1914 for Hamad Al-Saqr, was commanded by Abdulwahab Al-Qatami and ended her days buried under the rubble of Karachi port. Samhan, built in 1917 by Hajji Salman, was commanded by Abdulwahab and Yousef, sons of Eissa. The pages include a photograph of Bader in naval uniform, a portrait of Abdulwahab bin Eissa and his 1938 certificate of navigation, and Villiers' photographs.

Why it matters

The fullest published history of the family's ships.
