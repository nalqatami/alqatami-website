1902 · Signed letter, shown by the Al-Abduljaleel family

Nakhuda Eissa Al-Qatami's signature on his 1902 letter to Bombay

Eissa Abdulwahab Al-QatamiSager Abdulwahab Al-Qatami

What it is

A letter Eissa sent in 1902 to the merchant Ahmad Al-Abduljaleel in Bombay, telling him the ship had reached Muscat. The Al-Abduljaleel family kept the letter and shared his signature: «عيسى بن عبدالوهاب قطامي».

Why it matters

It is the oldest thing we have in Eissa's own hand. He was about 32 and already sailing to India and Muscat. The family that kept it says Nakhuda Sager Al-Qatami told them he had never seen a document in Eissa's handwriting.

Who is meant

He signs with his full name, Eissa bin Abdulwahab Qatami, and the caption names his books and his death in Muscat in 1929, which match our Eissa exactly.

Original text

التوقيع: عيسى بن عبدالوهاب قطامي تعليق الناشر: توقيع النوخذة القدير عيسى القطامي على رسالة أرسلها إلى الجد أحمد العبدالجليل في بومبي عام ١٩٠٢ ويخبره فيها بوصول السفينة إلى مسكت (مسقط). والنوخذة عيسى القطامي من أشهر وأمهر نواخذة السفن الشراعي، وألف العديد من الكتب التي أصبحت مرجعا لأهل الغوص والسفر منها كتاب «دليل المحتار في علم البحار» و«الخالص من كل عيب لوضع الجيب». توفي رحمه الله في مسقط (عمان) عام ١٩٢٩. وتعد الرسالة نادرة جدا لأن المرحوم النوخذة صقر القطامي أخبرني أنه لم يشاهد وثيقة بخط يد النوخذة عيسى القطامي.

English translation

Signature: Eissa bin Abdulwahab Qatami The poster's caption: The signature of the skilled Nakhuda Eissa Al-Qatami on a letter he sent to my grandfather Ahmad Al-Abduljaleel in Bombay in 1902, telling him the ship had arrived in Muscat. Nakhuda Eissa Al-Qatami was one of the best known and most skilled captains of sailing ships, and wrote several books that became references for pearl divers and seafarers, among them «Dalil Al-Muhtar fi Ilm Al-Bihar» and «Al-Khalis min Kul Aib li Wadh Al-Jaib». He died in Muscat, Oman, in 1929. The letter is very rare, because the late Nakhuda Sager Al-Qatami told me he had never seen a document in Nakhuda Eissa Al-Qatami's handwriting.

Notes

Only the signature has been published, not the full letter. The date and contents come from the Al-Abduljaleel family's caption.
