Masjid Ibn Qatami in the register of mosques built by the Awqaf Department, 1953

Malka Mohammad Al-Ghanim

What it is

The department’s table of «mosques established by the department within Kuwait City» lists, as no. 22, Masjid Ibn Qatami, dated 10 Dhul Hijja 1372 AH and 31 August 1953, at a cost of 66,924 rupees. This is the rebuilding of Masjid Al-Qatami in Sharq, which the Kawkaba min Al-Ruwwad pages date to 10 Dhul Hijja 1372.

Why it matters

The official record of the 1953 rebuilding of the family mosque, with its date and cost.
