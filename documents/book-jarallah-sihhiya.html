Tarikh Al-Khadamat Al-Sihhiya fil-Kuwait: Abdulwahab bin Eissa Al-Qatami on how the divers treated the sick

Abdulwahab Eissa Al-QatamiEissa Abdulwahab Al-Qatami

What it is

Page 41, «Medical treatment among the divers», draws on «a useful study written by Abdulwahab bin Eissa Al-Qatami in his book Al-Sayd wal-Tanaqqul wal-Tijara fil-Bihar» and quotes him: the divers’ treatment did not go beyond simple methods, and he lists four: the purge (senna), cauterising with fire, written amulets, and certain salts for skin cracked by sea water. The footnote cites his book, third printing 1964, pages 210 to 213, within Dalil Al-Muhtar fi Ilm Al-Bihar by Eissa Al-Qatami. The bibliography on page 290 lists «Abdulwahab bin Eissa Al-Qatami: Al-Sayd wal-Tanaqqul wal-Tijara fil-Bihar, Kuwait 1968».

Why it matters

A history of Kuwait’s health services uses Abdulwahab bin Eissa Al-Qatami’s writing as its source on medicine at sea.
