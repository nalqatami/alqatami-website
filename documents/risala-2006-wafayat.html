Deaths of notables from Ahmed Al-Bishr Al-Roumi’s papers: Yousef bin Eissa Al-Qatami, Vienna, 1981

Yousef Eissa Al-Qatami

What it is

«Wafayat Al-A'yan», the deaths of Kuwaiti notables recorded in Ahmed Al-Bishr Al-Roumi’s diaries. Page 13: «Yousef bin Eissa Al-Qatami died in Vienna on Tuesday 3 March 1981». Page 14 repeats it in the alphabetical list: Yousef bin Eissa Al-Qatami, 3/3/1981.

Why it matters

A printed record of the date and place of Yousef bin Eissa Al-Qatami’s death.
