1967 · British Embassy letter on the National Assembly

Rumours of Jassim Al-Qatami joining the Government

Jassim Abdulaziz Al-Qatami

What it is

With calls in the press to dissolve the Assembly and to bring opposition figures into the Government, the Embassy notes wild rumours that Jasim al Qatami and Dr Ahmad Khatib would be included.

Why it matters

It shows how central Jassim remained to Kuwaiti politics even outside the Assembly.

Who is meant

The same Jassim Al-Qatami as the records that give his full name: Director of Police, resigned at Suez, later a nationalist member of the National Assembly.

Page copies

Original text

The strengthening of the Government with some members of the 'opposition' has also been advocated and all sorts of wild rumours about the inclusion of Jasim al Qatami and Dr. Ahmad Khatib have been going the rounds.

English translation

كما طُرحت فكرة تقوية الحكومة ببعض أعضاء «المعارضة»، وتدور كل أنواع الشائعات الجامحة عن ضم جاسم القطامي ود. أحمد الخطيب.

Others named
