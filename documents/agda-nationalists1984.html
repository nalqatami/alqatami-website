1984 · British Embassy report on internal political affairs in Kuwait

Jassim Al-Qatami among the Nationalist candidates, 1984

Jassim Abdulaziz Al-Qatami

What it is

Ahead of the next elections, the Nationalists led by Ahmed Al-Khatib announce their candidates, among them former members Sami Al-Munayyes, Jassim Al-Qatami and Abdulla Al-Nibari.

Why it matters

Jassim still in the national movement nearly three decades after his police resignation.

Who is meant

The same Jassim Al-Qatami as the records that give his full name: Director of Police, resigned at Suez, later a nationalist member of the National Assembly.

Page copies

Original text

These include the Nationalists, led by Ahmed Al Khatib, ousted from the present Assembly in part by some clever boundary changes and pressure on voters from the Government. With Al Khatib will be ex-members Sami Al Munayyes, editor of radical magazine Al Tali'a, Jassim Al Qatami and Abdulla Al Naibari.

English translation

ومن هؤلاء القوميون بقيادة أحمد الخطيب، الذين أُبعدوا عن المجلس الحالي جزئيا بتعديلات ذكية في حدود الدوائر وبضغط حكومي على الناخبين. ومع الخطيب سيكون النواب السابقون سامي المنيس رئيس تحرير مجلة الطليعة الراديكالية، وجاسم القطامي، وعبدالله النيباري.
