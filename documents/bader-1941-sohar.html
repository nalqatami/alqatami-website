11 and 12 August 1941 · Two typed folios from a British tour report on the Batinah coast

A British officer's report from Sohar naming Bader bin Abdulwahab Al-Qatami

Bader Abdulwahab Al-Qatami

What it is

An officer sent to look for the pirate Mirza bin Berkat wrote that the Wali of Sohar had heard rumours that Mirza attacked «a sailing vessel belonged to one Budr bin Abdul Wahab Bin Qatami» on her way to Kuwait. The customs officer at Sohar added that «a man from Kuwait by name Budr bin Abdul Wahab Qatami came to Sohar in his own boat and purchased fire wood from many persons», and left on 2 July 1941. Word later came that the boat had been attacked and its owner and crew killed, and a Kuwaiti crew arriving a week before the report said his boat had not yet reached Kuwait.

Why it matters

It shows Bader as a nakhuda with his own boat, trading wood between the Batinah coast and Kuwait in 1941.

Who is meant

Bader Abdulwahab Al-Qatami, born about 1910, son of Abdulwahab Abdulaziz, who owned and sailed his own boats.

Page copies

Original text

Folio 30: He replied that there were rumours that Mirza attacked a sailing vessel belonged to one Budr bin Abdul Wahab Bin Qatami while she was enroute to Kuwait. Mirza said to have slaughtered the owner of the vessel, let the crew drown and looted the boat. Folio 31: The Amin of Sohar's customs Mr. Mahfoodh bin Khamis intimated to me that a man from Kuwait by name Budr bin Abdul Wahab Qatami came to Sohar in his own boat and purchased fire wood from many persons. After his departure which took place on the 2nd of July 1941, the Amin received information after sometimes that the boat was attacked by some pirates and looted. The owner of the boat and the crew were murdered. A week ago another sailing boat from Kuwait arrived at Sohar. The amin went to the crew and asked whether Budr had reached Kuwait or not. They replied that upto the date of their departure the boat had not arrived.

English translation

الورقة ٣٠: أجاب بأن هناك إشاعات بأن ميرزا هاجم سفينة شراعية لبدر بن عبدالوهاب بن قطامي وهي في طريقها إلى الكويت، ويقال إن ميرزا ذبح صاحب السفينة وترك البحارة يغرقون ونهب السفينة. الورقة ٣١: أخبرني أمين جمرك صحار السيد محفوظ بن خميس أن رجلا من الكويت اسمه بدر بن عبدالوهاب قطامي جاء إلى صحار في سفينته واشترى الحطب من كثيرين. وبعد مغادرته في ٢ يوليو ١٩٤١ وصل الأمين بعد مدة خبر بأن السفينة هاجمها قراصنة ونهبوها، وقُتل صاحبها وبحارتها. وقبل أسبوع وصلت صحار سفينة شراعية أخرى من الكويت، فسأل الأمين بحارتها هل وصل بدر إلى الكويت، فأجابوا بأن السفينة لم تكن قد وصلت حتى تاريخ مغادرتهم.

Notes

The killing was only a rumour. Bader lived until 1 January 1986.
