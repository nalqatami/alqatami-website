Khalid Tu'ma, Al-Kamil fi Tarikh Al-Kuwait: the Al-Qatami family and Eissa Al-Qatami on pearl diving

Eissa Abdulwahab Al-Qatami

What it is

1. Page 286 lists Al-Qatami among the families of Kuwait, and among the families who trace their descent to Anaza, with Al-Sabah, Al-Ghanim, Al-Roumi and others. 2. Page 291: «Eissa Al-Qatami says, on the kinds and names of pearl diving boats», followed on page 292 by his list of the boat types, the battil, the baqqara, the sambuk, the shu'i, the jalibut and the boom, with their names, footnoted to Dalil Al-Muhtar fi 'Ilm Al-Bihar, fourth edition, 1976, Kuwait Government Press, page 211. 3. Page 293: «Eissa Al-Qatami says: the diving season begins at the start of Ayyar (May) and ends in Ayloul (September), so the diving season lasts four months», footnoted to Dalil Al-Muhtar, page 216.

Why it matters

A recent history of Kuwait that counts Al-Qatami among the old families and quotes Eissa Al-Qatami's Dalil Al-Muhtar as an authority on the pearl diving boats and season.

Who is meant

Eissa Al-Qatami, author of Dalil Al-Muhtar, is Eissa Abdulwahab Al-Qatami. Page 286 names the family only, not a person.
