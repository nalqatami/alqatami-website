22 February 1961 (printed 5 March 1961) · Kuwait Al-Youm, issue 317

Abdulwahab Abdulaziz Al-Qatami elected to the first board of Gulf Bank

Abdulwahab Abdulaziz Al-Qatami

What it is

The minutes of the founding general assembly of Gulf Bank, held at the Mubarakiya School on 22 February 1961, list the votes for the first board. Abdulwahab Abdulaziz Al-Qatami is among the twelve elected.

Why it matters

It shows him not only founding the bank but sitting on its first board.
