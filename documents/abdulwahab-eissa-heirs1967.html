Heirs register of Abdulwahab bin Eissa Al-Qatami

What it is

Inheritance register 111 records that Abdulwahab bin Eissa bin Abdulwahab Al-Qatami died on 15 December 1967. His heirs were his wife Amna bint Abdulaziz Al-Qatami, his daughters Hessa, Sabeeka, Rugaya, Lolwa, Monira, Sharifa and Ghanima, and his full brother Yousef.

Why it matters

It gives the exact date of grandpa Abdulwahab Eissa's death, which we did not have before, and confirms his wife and daughters as they appear in the tree.

Who is meant

Abdulwahab bin Eissa bin Abdulwahab Al-Qatami, his wife Amna bint Abdulaziz and his brother Yousef are all in our tree.

English translation

Inheritance register 111, dated 7 February 1968: "Abdulwahab bin Eissa bin Abdulwahab Al-Qatami died on 15 December 1967, survived by his wife Amna bint Abdulaziz Al-Qatami, his daughters by her Hessa, Sabeeka, Rugaya, Lolwa, Monira, Sharifa and Ghanima, and his full brother Yousef."

Notes

Ma'alim part 7, book page 103, plot 101. The book quotes the register; we have not seen the register itself.
