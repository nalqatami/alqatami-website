1911 to 1918 · Account statements and letters of the Kuwaiti merchant Mohammed bin Abdullah Al-Matrook

Abdulaziz bin Qatami in the Al-Matrook trading ledgers

Abdulaziz Abdulwahab Abdulaziz Al-Qatami

What it is

Abdulaziz bin Qatami appears in about 30 ledgers and letters of the merchant Mohammed bin Abdullah Al-Matrook between 1911 and 1918. In a 1912 letter to Karachi he is due to sail on the next ship with Abdulaziz Al-Saud and Al-Othman. Another 1912 letter has him travelling with Khalid bin Khamis, and a 1915 ledger records goods held with him.

Why it matters

It shows Jassim's father as an active merchant in the trade between Kuwait, Basra and India in the years before the First World War.

Who is meant

Confirmed by the family: Abdulaziz bin Qatami in Kuwait records of this period is Abdulaziz bin Abdulwahab, Jassim's father. A 1918 ledger also names his son as Abdulwahab bin Abdulaziz bin Qatami.

Original text

رسالة رقم ٣٨٧، ١٩١٢م، من محمد بن عبدالله بن متروك (الكويت) إلى خالد بن فايز بن خميس (كراتشي): الاسماء: عبدالعزيز السعود / عبدالعزيز بن قطامي / عبدالعزيز بن عثمان من المضمون: بانه عبدالعزيز السعود و القطامي و العثمان سيتوجهون في المركب القادم رسالة رقم ٤٤٤، ١٩١٢م، إلى محمد ثنيان الغانم: من المضمون: ومعه عبدالعزيز بن قطامي و عبداللطيف بن عيسى وثيقة رقم ١٠٨ ك ٨، كشف حساب ١٩١٥م: عند عبدالعزيز بن قطامي قوطي ودج

English translation

Letter no. 387, 1912, from Mohammed bin Abdullah bin Matrook (Kuwait) to Khalid bin Fayez bin Khamis (Karachi): Names: Abdulaziz Al-Saud / Abdulaziz bin Qatami / Abdulaziz bin Othman From the contents: that Abdulaziz Al-Saud, Al-Qatami and Al-Othman will travel on the next ship. Letter no. 444, 1912, to Mohammed Thunayan Al-Ghanim: From the contents: and with him Abdulaziz bin Qatami and Abdullatif bin Eissa. Document 108, book 8, account statement, 1915: With Abdulaziz bin Qatami: a tin (qouti) of wadaj.

Notes

Alwathaeq publishes these ledgers as scans with typed extracts of the names and items, not full readings. The lines above are their extracts. All 28 ledgers that name him are saved in the family Drive.
