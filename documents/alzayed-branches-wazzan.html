Al-Qatami named among the branches of Al-Zayed

Abdulaziz Al-Qatami

What it is

In the first chapter of his study of Hamad Al-Saqr, Dr Faisal Al-Wazzan lists the families that branch from Al-Zayed: Al-Ghanim, Thunayan Al-Ghanim, Shaheen Al-Ghanim, Jasser Al-Ghanim, Jabr Al-Ghanim, Saqer Al-Ghanim, Al-Qatami, Al-Githeebi, Al-Yacoub, Al-Ghunaim, Al-Zayed, Al-Majed, Al-Binwan and Al-Saqr of Bahrain. He says Al-Zayed belong to the Utub alliance, mostly trace to Anaza, and moved with Al-Khalifa from Kuwait to Al-Zubara in 1766 before returning to Kuwait.

Why it matters

A second published source, independent of the Al Zayed book, that places Al-Qatami among the Al-Zayed families alongside Al-Ghanim, the same line given in Ma’alim part 7.

Who is meant

The family is named directly: القطامي.

Original text

أما أسرة آل زايد التي تتفرع منها أسرة الصقر، فهي تضم أيضاً أسر الغانم، ثنيان الغانم، شاهين الغانم، جاسر الغانم، جبر الغانم، صقر الغانم، القطامي، القضيبي، اليعقوب، الغنيم، الزايد، الماجد، البنوان، والصقر (البحرين)، وغيرهم. وتعد أسرة الماجد الأكثر قربا من الصقر من حيث النسب، حيث تجتمعان في الجد محمد بن صقر بن غانم.

English translation

As for the Al-Zayed family, from which the Al-Saqr family branches, it also includes the families of Al-Ghanim, Thunayan Al-Ghanim, Shaheen Al-Ghanim, Jasser Al-Ghanim, Jabr Al-Ghanim, Saqer Al-Ghanim, Al-Qatami, Al-Githeebi, Al-Yacoub, Al-Ghunaim, Al-Zayed, Al-Majed, Al-Binwan and Al-Saqr (Bahrain), among others. The Al-Majed family is the closest to Al-Saqr in descent, as the two meet in the ancestor Mohammed bin Saqr bin Ghanim.

Notes

Book page 13. The author cites Yousef Mohammed Saqr Al-Jasser Al-Ghanim, «Al Zayed: Tarikh wa Rijal», April 2000, an unpublished study, for the Al-Zayed move to Bahrain.
