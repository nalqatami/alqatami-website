Before 1870 (published 2024) · Lineage note in Ma'alim part 7, on the Al-Qatami house plot

Two marriages in the lineage: Abdulwahab bin Abdulaziz to Lolwa bint Mohammed Ghanim Al-Ghanim, and Eissa bin Abdulwahab to Moza bint Shaheen Al-Ghanim

What it is

Writing about the Al-Qatami house in Fereej Ibn Rumi (plot 42), the book says the house first belonged to Abdulaziz bin Ghanim bin Abdullah bin Ibrahim bin Mohammed Al-Ghanim Al-Zayed, known as Al-Qatami. His son Abdulwahab married Lolwa bint Mohammed Ghanim Al-Ghanim, and their children were Abdulaziz, Eissa, Sharifa and Mariam. It then lists the children of Abdulaziz, and says Eissa married Moza bint Shaheen Al-Ghanim and had Abdulwahab, Yousef and Aisha.

Why it matters

This is the published, sourced record of both marriages. It matches our tree exactly: the same husbands, Lolwa's four children Abdulaziz, Eissa, Sharifa and Mariam, Moza's three children Abdulwahab, Yousef and Aisha, and the same Al-Zayed line given in the Al Zayed book.

Who is meant

The note is written about the Al-Qatami house plot itself and gives the full Al-Zayed line, so there is no doubt it is our family.

Original text

البيت في الأساس ملك عبدالعزيز بن غانم بن عبدالله بن إبراهيم بن محمد الغانم الزايد والذي لقب بـ «القطامي»... وعبدالوهاب بن عبدالعزيز تزوج لولوة بنت محمد غانم الغانم، وله من الأبناء (عبدالعزيز وعيسى وشريفة ومريم). ابنه عبدالعزيز له من الأبناء (عبدالوهاب وعبدالرحمن ومحمد وجاسم وعبدالله وأحمد وآمنة وفاطمة ومريم وموزه ولولوة وحصة وعائشة)، وقد تزوج أكثر من زوجة وهم: رقية بنت عبدالرحمن بن الشيخ فرج (والدة عبدالوهاب وعبدالرحمن وآمنة وفاطمة)، وعائشة بنت أحمد بن عبدالعزيز العريفان والدة باقي الأبناء. أما عيسى فقد تزوج موزه بنت شاهين الغانم وله من الأولاد (عبدالوهاب ويوسف وعائشة).

English translation

The house originally belonged to Abdulaziz bin Ghanim bin Abdullah bin Ibrahim bin Mohammed Al-Ghanim Al-Zayed, who was given the name «Al-Qatami»... Abdulwahab bin Abdulaziz married Lolwa bint Mohammed Ghanim Al-Ghanim, and his children were Abdulaziz, Eissa, Sharifa and Mariam. His son Abdulaziz had Abdulwahab, Abdulrahman, Mohammed, Jassim, Abdullah, Ahmad, Amna, Fatima, Mariam, Moza, Lolwa, Hessa and Aisha. He married more than one wife: Rugaya bint Abdulrahman bin Al-Sheikh Faraj (mother of Abdulwahab, Abdulrahman, Amna and Fatima), and Aisha bint Ahmad bin Abdulaziz Al-Oraifan, mother of the rest. As for Eissa, he married Moza bint Shaheen Al-Ghanim and had Abdulwahab, Yousef and Aisha.

Notes

Ma'alim part 7, book page 79, footnote 42. This is the authors' genealogy note, written from their research for the book. It is not a copy of a marriage contract. The family knows that it was his son Abdulwahab, not Abdulaziz himself, who was first called Al-Qatami.
