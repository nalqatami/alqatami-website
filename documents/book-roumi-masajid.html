Masjid Al-Qatami, named for the house of Abdulaziz Al-Qatami, in Adnan Al-Roumi's Tarikh Masajid Al-Dira Al-Qadima

Malka Mohammad Al-Ghanim

What it is

The entry on Masjid Al-Qatami (pages 41 to 45). Its name is that of Malka bint Mohammed Al-Ghanim; its popular name is «Bin Qatami», «because it is close to the house of Abdulaziz Al-Qatami, so it was called Masjid Al-Qatami». It stood in Sharq, in Fereej Al-Shamlan (or Al-Roumi), between Fereej Bu Qmaz and Fereej Al-Asousi, behind the Al-Roumi building, «and near it the house of Abdulaziz Al-Qatami»; today it is near the Ministry of Public Health and the Al-Shamlan diwan, facing Gulf Street, and it still stands. Malka bint Mohammed Al-Jabr Al-Ghanim founded it near her house so that her husband, Mohammed bin Saqr bin Ghanim bin Jabr Al-Ghanim, could pray there after he grew heavy; Al-Nabhani names Sultan bin Majid as founder. It was founded in 1250 AH (1834). The Awqaf Department first renovated it at a cost of 66,924 rupees, completed on 10 Dhu Al-Hijja 1372 (20 August 1953). Its imams were Mulla Mohammed bin Hussein bin Abdullah Al-Turkait, his son Sheikh Mohammed Saleh bin Mohammed Al-Turkait, Sheikh Mohammed bin Mohammed Saleh Al-Turkait, then Mubarak bin Bishr bin Yousef Al-Roumi; its muezzins included Sultan Boursli. Scholars who visited Shamlan bin Ali bin Saif Al-Roumi taught in it. Malka endowed two houses near the mosque, one a school for memorising the Quran and the other the home of Sheikh Mohammed bin Hussein Al-Turkait and his son, and she served the mosque all her life, followed by her children. A plate shows the mosque with its minaret.

Why it matters

The fullest published entry on the family mosque: it says the mosque took the name Al-Qatami from the house of Abdulaziz Al-Qatami beside it, and records its founding, renovation, imams and waqf.
