1957 · English translation, kept in a British file, of an Arabic article about Kuwait

An Arab visitor's account: the Chief of Police resigns

Jassim Abdulaziz Al-Qatami

What it is

An Arab writer describes Kuwait's politics, the Clubs Federation's leadership in the Port Said crisis, and the general strike. He says that when the police were ordered to fire on demonstrators, the Chief of Police, Jassim al-Qatami, resigned.

Why it matters

It gives the reason for Jassim's resignation as it was told in the Arab press: he would not have the police fire on demonstrators.

Who is meant

The same Jassim Al-Qatami as the records that give his full name: Director of Police, resigned at Suez, later a nationalist member of the National Assembly.

Page copies

Original text

Political, cultural and social activities have crystallized into the clubs controlled by a Federation which took over popular leadership during the Port Said crisis. It called for a general strike, a boycott of British goods, and for the expulsion of all British officials from the Kuwaiti Government. The strike was a success. When the police were ordered to fire on demonstrators the Chief of Police, Jassim al-Qatami, resigned his post.

English translation

تبلورت النشاطات السياسية والثقافية والاجتماعية في الأندية التي يديرها اتحاد تولى القيادة الشعبية خلال أزمة بورسعيد. ودعا الاتحاد إلى إضراب عام ومقاطعة البضائع البريطانية وطرد جميع الموظفين البريطانيين من حكومة الكويت. ونجح الإضراب. وعندما صدرت الأوامر للشرطة بإطلاق النار على المتظاهرين، استقال رئيس الشرطة جاسم القطامي من منصبه.

Notes

This passage is itself an English translation of an Arabic article, so the Arabic here is a translation back, not the writer's own words.
