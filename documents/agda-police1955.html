21 June 1955 · Letter from the British Political Agency in Kuwait to the Foreign Office

Jassim Al-Qatami, Director of the Police Department, visits London

Jassim Abdulaziz Al-Qatami

What it is

The Agency tells London that Jassim, the Kuwaiti Director of the Police Department, is on three months' leave in Europe. It credits him, with Sheikh Saad, for the growing efficiency of the police, and notes he was educated mainly in Egypt and studied police work in England before taking over as Director.

Why it matters

The earliest record found of Jassim Abdulaziz Al-Qatami in office, a year before his famous resignation, with details of his education.

Who is meant

The same Jassim Al-Qatami as the records that give his full name: Director of Police, resigned at Suez, later a nationalist member of the National Assembly.

Page copies

Original text

Political Agency, Kuwait. Restricted. June 21, 1955. Dear Christopher, Jasim Qatami, the Kuwaiti Director of the Police Department, has gone to Europe for three months' leave and he is expected to arrive in London about now. He did not tell us that he was going to visit England nor did he ask us to arrange any facilities for him but he is a good friend of ours and has been largely responsible with Shaikh Sa'd for the growing efficiency of the Police Department and its increasing readiness to co-operate with us. You may remember that he was largely educated in Egypt but that he spent some months in England studying police work and methods before returning to Kuwait to take over as Director in the Police Department …

English translation

الوكالة السياسية، الكويت. مقيد. ٢١ يونيو ١٩٥٥. عزيزي كريستوفر، سافر جاسم القطامي، المدير الكويتي لدائرة الشرطة، إلى أوروبا في إجازة مدتها ثلاثة أشهر، ومن المتوقع أن يصل إلى لندن في هذه الأيام. لم يخبرنا بأنه سيزور إنجلترا ولم يطلب منا ترتيب أي تسهيلات له، لكنه صديق جيد لنا، وهو مسؤول إلى حد كبير مع الشيخ سعد عن تزايد كفاءة دائرة الشرطة واستعدادها المتزايد للتعاون معنا. ولعلك تذكر أنه تلقى معظم تعليمه في مصر، لكنه قضى بضعة أشهر في إنجلترا يدرس العمل الشرطي وأساليبه قبل عودته إلى الكويت ليتولى إدارة دائرة الشرطة …
