26 Shawwal 1320 AH, 25 January 1903 · Sale and endowment deed, sealed by Judge Mohammed bin Abdullah Al-Adsani in the time of Sheikh Mubarak Al-Sabah

Waqf deed of Bazza bint Noura Al-Quayan

What it is

Fatima bint Musa Aba Al-Kubood sells a house to Aisha bint Abdulaziz ibn Qatami for 180 riyals. The house lies beside the enclosure of Duaij bin Fahad, with open roads on its east and south sides. Aisha bought it for her mother, Bazza bint Noura Al-Quayan. Bazza then made the house a permanent endowment: each year a sacrifice and food for the poor in her name and her mother's, and the house itself for her daughter Aisha, then for Aisha's children Abdulaziz, Bazza and Latifa, children of Saad bin Ghanim bin Saad, and their descendants.

Why it matters

It records a daughter of the family, Aisha bint Abdulaziz Al-Qatami, buying property in her own name in 1903, and her marriage into the Al-Ghanim family through Saad bin Ghanim bin Saad. Notes added later in the margins record the house being divided among the heirs.

English translation

Adsani deed dated 26 Shawwal 1320 AH / 25 January 1903 Sealed by the judge Mohammed bin Abdullah Al-Adsani Seller: Fatima bint Musa Aba Al-Kubood Buyer: Aisha bint Abdulaziz ibn Qatami The house was bought specifically for her mother, Bazza bint Noura (Al-Quayan). Then Bazza endowed the house, after her, to her descendants Abdulaziz and his sisters Bazza and Latifa, children of Saad ibn Ghanim ibn Saad, and after them to their descendants. The property: the house bounded on the qibla side by the enclosure of Duaij bin Fahad, on the north by the house of (..), on the east by the open road and on the south by the open road. Price: 180 riyals Names mentioned: Mohammed ibn Abdullah Al-Adsani / Fatima bint Musa Aba Al-Kubood / Aisha bint Abdulaziz ibn Qatami / Duaij bin Fahad / her mother Bazza bint Noura (Al-Quayan) / Abdulaziz and his sisters Bazza and Latifa, children of Saad ibn Ghanim ibn Saad / Ahmad bin Saleh Al-Ghanim / Ahmad bin Mohammed Al-Ghanim Currencies in use: rupee / riyal Explanation and commentary: Mohammed Dhurgham Al-Matrook Clarification: Aisha Abdulaziz Al-Qatami bought the house for her mother Bazza bint Noura (Al-Quayan). Then Bazza endowed the house to her daughter Aisha and her children Abdulaziz, Latifa and Bazza, children of Saad Ghanim Saad Al-Ghanim. Ahmad bin Saleh Al-Ghanim, named in the later change to the endowment, is the son of Bazza Saad Al-Ghanim.
