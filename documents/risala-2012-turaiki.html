Jassim Al-Qatami on Abdullah Al-Turaiki and the oil participation agreement

Jassim Abdulaziz Al-Qatami

What it is

An article on the Saudi oil expert Abdullah Al-Turaiki and his advice to Kuwait, which reproduces his memorandum of October 1966 to Sheikh Jaber Al-Ahmad on pages 30 and 31. Page 32 says that Al-Turaiki took part in sessions on Arab affairs with Jassim Al-Qatami, Dr Ahmad Al-Khatib, Abdullatif Al-Hamad, Sami Al-Munayyis, Abdullah Zakaria Al-Ansari and others, and quotes Jassim Al-Qatami, through Mohammed Abdullah Al-Saif’s book, that Al-Turaiki’s ideas lay behind the oil participation agreement the National Assembly approved, and that Kuwait’s development owes much to him.

Why it matters

Jassim Al-Qatami’s own view of the oil participation agreement, which the British Embassy letter of 1973 on this site also records.
