1907 · Oral history quoted in a documentary study

Nakhuda Abdulaziz Al-Qatami commands Kuwait's first steamship, Sheikh Mubarak's yacht Mushrif

Abdulaziz Abdulwahab Abdulaziz Al-Qatami

What it is

Sheikh Mubarak Al-Sabah bought a steamship from Bombay in 1907 for 100,000 rupees and named it «Yakht Mushrif». The book says it was the first steamship bought in Kuwait and that it was commanded by Nakhuda Abdulaziz Al-Qatami. The same page prints Sheikh Mubarak's letter of 30 Jumada II 1331 (6 June 1913) to Saqr Al-Abdullah Al-Saqr about the yacht's fittings.

Why it matters

It puts Abdulaziz at the wheel of the ruler's own yacht, the first steamship in Kuwait. Al-Hajji's book also says he was called to Bombay to bring Mushrif back.

Who is meant

In 1907 the nakhuda Abdulaziz Al-Qatami was Abdulaziz bin Abdulwahab, Eissa's brother. Al-Hajji names him as the captain called to Bombay to bring Mushrif back.

Page copies

Original text

وكان أول من اشترى باخرة الشيخ مبارك بمبلغ ١٠٠ ألف روبية جلبها من بومبي، قادها النوخذة عبدالعزيز القطامي، وكان ذلك سنة ١٩٠٧م، أطلق عليها تسمية «يخت مشرف».

English translation

The first to buy a steamship was Sheikh Mubarak, for 100,000 rupees, brought from Bombay and commanded by Nakhuda Abdulaziz Al-Qatami. That was in 1907, and he named it «Yakht Mushrif».

Notes

Book page 263. The author's source for the yacht is Abdullah Al-Hatem's «Min Huna Bada'at Al-Kuwait», p. 15, and a 1985 television interview with Khalid Abdullatif Al-Hamad. The sentence is grammatically loose in the book; the meaning is that Sheikh Mubarak was the first to buy a steamship.
