1957 · British Embassy letter on commercial relations between Kuwait and the Soviet Union

Jassim Al-Qatami, Managing Director of the Kuwait Cinema Company

Jassim Abdulaziz Al-Qatami

What it is

Lists young Kuwaiti businessmen interested in trade with the Soviet bloc. Jassim Qatami is named as Managing Director of the Kuwait Cinema Company and a partner in Equipment Limited, agents for British firms.

Why it matters

It records Jassim's business life after the police: the Kuwait Cinema Company and Equipment Limited.

Who is meant

The same Jassim Al-Qatami as the records that give his full name: Director of Police, resigned at Suez, later a nationalist member of the National Assembly.

Page copies

Original text

4. Three other young men may also be numbered among those who have shown considerable interest in Soviet bloc trade. These are Jassim Qatami who is the Managing Director of the Kuwait Cinema Company and was formerly Director of Police. He resigned from this latter post during the Suez demonstrations of last November. Qatami is also a partner of Equipment Limited who have a number of British agencies including that for Sankey Sheldon, manufacturers of steel office furniture.

English translation

٤. يمكن أيضا أن يُعدّ ثلاثة شبان آخرين من بين من أبدوا اهتماما كبيرا بالتجارة مع الكتلة السوفيتية، وهم جاسم القطامي، العضو المنتدب لشركة السينما الكويتية ومدير الشرطة السابق، وقد استقال من منصبه هذا خلال مظاهرات السويس في نوفمبر الماضي. والقطامي أيضا شريك في شركة المعدات المحدودة التي تملك عددا من الوكالات البريطانية، منها وكالة سانكي شيلدون لصناعة الأثاث المكتبي الفولاذي.
