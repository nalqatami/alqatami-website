6 February 1964 (23 Ramadan 1383) · Handwritten letter on his own letterhead, Arabian Gulf Street, cable «Badri»

Letter from Abdulwahab Abdulaziz Al-Qatami to Nakhuda Mohammed Ali Al-Tankisiri

Abdulwahab Abdulaziz Al-Qatami

What it is

He tells the nakhuda that his letter and cable arrived, that his brother has gone to Basra because the money is ready, and to come on to Basra. He signs with greetings to all.

Why it matters

His cable address «Badri» was the name of his ship Al-Badri.
