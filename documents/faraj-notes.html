What it is

The notes record that Ruqaya, daughter of Abdulrahman Al-Faraj, married Abdulaziz bin Qatami, and that their son is Abdulwahab bin Abdulaziz Al-Qatami.

Why it matters

It confirms Abdulwahab Abdulaziz's mother from her own family's papers.
