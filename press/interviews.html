Interviews

  1. «The first Kuwaiti police officer: Jassim Abdulaziz Al-Qatami.» Asked when he was born, he answers: «In about 1927, because in our days there was no birth certificate to prove it, so I am now 26, and God knows best.» He describes four years at the Egyptian Police College, how Abdulaziz Hussein, then supervisor of Kuwait's missions in Egypt, first suggested it to him, his coming year of training at Scotland Yard in criminal investigation and fingerprints, his 1951 study visits to the police of Italy, Switzerland, France and England, and says police must see themselves as servants of the people.
    1 June 1953 · Al-Raed magazine, «Al-Awa'il» (The Firsts), pages 241 to 244 (copy on Kazima) · Jassim Abdulaziz Al-Qatami
  2. His daughter Khalda Al-Qatami tells his story: born and raised in Sharq in 1922, son of Abdulaziz Al-Qatami and nephew of Eissa Al-Qatami. He travelled with his brother Abdulwahab bin Abdulaziz and his cousin Abdulwahab bin Eissa to India, Zanzibar and Mombasa, and later urged Kuwaitis to invest whenever he saw a good opportunity in Dubai. Part of a feature on four men «who helped raise Kuwait».
    2 November 2008 · Al-Anba · Ahmed Abdulaziz Al-Qatami
  3. «I am from a family of nakhudas and people of the sea.» Nakhuda Sager Abdulwahab Al-Qatami, youngest son of Nakhuda Abdulwahab bin Abdulaziz, born in Fereej Al-Mutabba in Sharq in 1929, went to sea at 14. He lists the family's captains: Eissa, author of Dalil Al-Muhtar; Abdulwahab Eissa of the boom Ibn Rashdan; Yousef Eissa; Abdulaziz, captain of Al-Badri, whom Sheikh Mubarak asked to bring his yacht Mushrif from Bombay; his father Abdulwahab, a nakhuda at 20; Abdulrahman; Abdullah; Mohammed Abdulaziz of the family boom Al-Hisan; Bader, who trained under his brother and commanded Al-Sulaimani; and Naser, «born in the year of the Battle of Jahra». He tells of his father commanding Al-Daw in 1941 and the visit to the King of Yemen at Hodeidah, a Japanese submarine attack near Goa in 1944, his father leaving Al-Badri to Bader in 1936, and his own book Dalil Al-Bahr.
    16 January 2009 · Al-Qabas (copy on the Kuwait History forum) · Sager Abdulwahab Al-Qatami
  4. «I went to sea at 14, aboard the most famous booms.» A longer interview in parts: his first voyage with his father on Al-Daw, two years with his brother Naser on Al-Badri, then with his brother Yousef; his Grade A and B nakhuda licence of 12 April 1961; how Al-Badri was sold in Aden to the Bazara family after more than seventy years of service; and being chosen to go to India for timber to rebuild the boom Al-Mohallab for the late Amir Sheikh Jaber Al-Ahmad.
    25 January 2012 · Al-Qabas, «Bahr wa Sayd» series, by Abdullah Eissa (copy on the Kuwait History forum) · Sager Abdulwahab Al-Qatami
