About 1904 to 1954

A family of nakhudas

Dr Yaqub Al-Hajji opens his chapter on the family with these words: «Al-Qatami is a family distinguished by its men's ties to sea travel and the command of sailing ships, and by the number of famous nakhudas who came from it.» Most of the men commanded ships from the decade before the First World War until the end of Kuwaiti sail. They did not work in pearl diving. They sailed the long trading voyages to India, Yemen and East Africa.

He adds that the men encouraged and taught one another, and that this is how the family kept its place among the great nakhudas. Al-Hajji knew several of them himself and recorded their stories in person, above all Nakhuda Bader Abdulwahab, who left on him «a good impression the years have not erased».
