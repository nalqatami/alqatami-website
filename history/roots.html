Roots

Al-Zayed, the Al-Ghanim line, and the name Al-Qatami

Abdulaziz Al-Qatami

The family descends from Abdulaziz bin Ghanim bin Abdullah bin Ibrahim bin Mohammed Al-Ghanim Al-Zayed. Every Al-Qatami in this tree comes from him. The Landmarks of Old Kuwait Town, writing about the family house beside the mosque in Fereej Ibn Rumi, says Abdulaziz himself was given the name Al-Qatami, but the family has always known that it was his son Abdulwahab who was first called Al-Qatami, and that is why the oldest deed, from 1863, speaks of «the house of Abdulwahab ibn Qatami».

The Al Zayed book by Yousef Mohammed Saqr Al-Jasser Al-Ghanim (2006) gives the full chart. Its Al-Qatami page opens with Abdulaziz bin Ghanim bin Abdullah bin Ibrahim bin Mohammed bin Ghanim bin Jabr bin Ali bin Ghanim Al-Zayed, then his son Abdulwahab, then Abdulwahab's sons Abdulaziz and Eissa, and every branch below them. On the page before, Ghanim's sons are shown as Abdulaziz, whom the book marks as the first Al-Qatami, Eissa, and Mohammed, the father of Thunayan, Ahmed, Ibrahim, Shaheen and Jassim Al-Ghanim. Al-Qatami and Al-Ghanim are one house, divided only by a name.

Dr Faisal Al-Wazzan, in his 2019 study of Hamad Al-Saqr, lists Al-Qatami among the families that branch from Al-Zayed, together with Al-Ghanim, Thunayan Al-Ghanim, Shaheen Al-Ghanim, Jabr Al-Ghanim, Al-Ghunaim and Al-Saqr. He writes that Al-Zayed belong to the Utub. His earlier book, Kuwait and the Utub in the documents, oral accounts and Arab and foreign writings, 1563 to 1814 (2018), also lists Al-Qatami in its chart of the Al-Zayed branches, mainly between Kuwait and Bahrain. So three published books give the same line.

The Landmarks of Old Kuwait Town adds that Al-Zayed came to Kuwait within the Utub alliance in the first quarter of the eighteenth century.

The same book traces where Al-Zayed came from. It places them in Al-Ali of the Dahamsha, of the Amarat of Anaza, and names Al-Qatami among the Kuwaiti families under the Al-Zayed name. In the droughts of the seventeenth century Al-Zayed left with Al-Jalahma, Al-Sabah and Al-Khalifa for Wadi Al-Haddar in Al-Aflaj in Najd. From there they formed the Utub alliance, moved to Al-Ahsa, lived about fifty years in Qatar, sailed to the Shatt Al-Arab, and settled in Kuwait in 1128 AH (1715) with the leave of the Bani Khalid emir. This agrees with what the family has always said, that we came from Najd. The book adds that when the Utub families shared out their responsibilities, the affairs of the sea fell to Al-Jalahma and Al-Zayed.
